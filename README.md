### Nearly Free electron
From the Central equation, we obtain the [band structure for weak potentials](https://github.com/Karmukilan-S/Condensed-matter/blob/main/Nearly%20free%20electron.py) numerically. [Assignment](https://github.com/Karmukilan-S/Condensed-matter/blob/main/CMP_project_3.pdf)

For $V(x)=A\cos(\frac{2\pi x}{a'})+\frac{A}{2}\cos(\frac{4\pi x}{a'})+\frac{A}{3}\cos(\frac{6\pi x}{a'})$ a'=2a obtaining the [Band structure, Density Of States](https://github.com/Karmukilan-S/Condensed-matter/blob/main/NFE_2.py) and [Convergence of Fermi energy for $n\rightarrow \infty$](https://github.com/Karmukilan-S/Condensed-matter/blob/main/E_tot.py) Numerically.[Assignment](https://github.com/Karmukilan-S/Condensed-matter/blob/main/CMP_project_4.pdf)
